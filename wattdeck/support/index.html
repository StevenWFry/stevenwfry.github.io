Common Questions
Which bikes work?
Any indoor bike that broadcasts the standard Bluetooth Fitness Machine Service (FTMS) for Indoor Bike Data. Most modern connected spin bikes do. If yours does not appear, it may not support FTMS.
My bike does not appear while scanning
Wake the bike by pedalling or pressing a console button, then scan again. Also check that no other app is already connected — most bikes allow only one active Bluetooth connection at a time, so a training app running in the background will hold the bike.
I see power, cadence and speed, but not distance or elapsed time
Some bikes leave those fields out of their FTMS packets. WattDeck estimates elapsed time and distance locally during an active workout so you still get a complete ride summary.
My ride did not appear in history
Rides shorter than a minute are treated as accidental and are not saved, so a stray start and stop does not clutter your history. Ride for at least a minute before stopping.
The power numbers look too high
Consumer bikes estimate power from cadence and resistance rather than measuring it with a strain gauge, and many read high. Use the power correction slider in settings to scale readings so they line up with a meter you trust.
Apple Health permission seems slow to appear
The system permission sheet can take a moment. The app shows a requesting state immediately while iOS prepares the prompt.
Heart-rate zones look wrong for me
Zone thresholds are adjustable in settings. The defaults are only a starting point.
Can WattDeck change my bike's resistance?
No. WattDeck reads your bike; it does not control it. Most bikes do not expose resistance control over Bluetooth.
