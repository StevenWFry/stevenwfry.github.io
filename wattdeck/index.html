wattdeck(1) Indoor Cycling Dashboard wattdeck(1)
Name
WattDeck — Bluetooth FTMS dashboard for indoor bikes
What It Is

WattDeck is an iPhone app for indoor cycling bikes that speak Bluetooth FTMS. It surfaces the ride data Apple’s Workout app does not read from FTMS — above all, live power.

During a ride it shows power, cadence, speed, heart rate, elapsed time, distance and calories, and charts power, cadence and heart rate as you go. Finished rides are stored on the device and can be written to Apple Health.

Compatibility

WattDeck works with indoor bikes that broadcast the standard Bluetooth Fitness Machine Service (FTMS) for Indoor Bike Data. Most modern connected spin bikes do.

What you see depends on what your bike actually advertises. Bikes that omit distance or elapsed time from their FTMS packets are handled by estimating those locally during a workout.

Heart rate is read from the bike, or from any standard Bluetooth heart rate monitor.

Features
Live ride metrics
Power, cadence, speed, heart rate, elapsed time, distance and calories in a high-contrast workout dashboard.
Live graphs
Power, cadence and heart rate charted across the last few minutes of the ride, so you can see your effort as a shape rather than a number.
Ride history
Every ride is kept on the device with its full recording, not just totals. Open a past ride to see the whole session charted from start to finish.
Apple Health export
Save completed rides to Apple Health as indoor cycling workouts, including duration, distance, active energy and heart-rate samples.
Power correction
Consumer bikes estimate power rather than measuring it, and many read high. A correction slider scales the readings to match a known-good meter.
Heart-rate zones
Set your own zone thresholds instead of accepting fixed defaults.
Privacy

No account, no sign-in, no server. WattDeck has no network access at all — your rides live on your device and in Apple Health, and nowhere else.
