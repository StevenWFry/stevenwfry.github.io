Name
WattDeck — privacy policy
Last updated: September 9, 2026
Summary

WattDeck has no network access. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Nothing you record is transmitted anywhere.

WattDeck does not run analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. The developer cannot see your rides, your Health data, or how you use the app.

Bluetooth Data

WattDeck reads workout data broadcast by your bike over the Bluetooth Fitness Machine Service and Heart Rate Service. This may include power, cadence, speed, distance, resistance, energy and heart rate.

Bluetooth is also used to discover nearby fitness machines so you can choose which one to connect to. This data is used only to operate the app on your device.

On-Device Storage

Completed rides are saved on your device. Each saved ride includes its summary — date, duration, distance, calories, and average and maximum power, cadence and heart rate — together with the recorded time series for the session.

Settings such as power correction, heart-rate zones, theme preference and rider profile values are also stored locally.

This information is kept in the app's own storage on your device. It is not backed up to a developer server, because there is no developer server.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, WattDeck writes completed rides to Apple Health as indoor cycling workouts, including duration, distance, active energy and heart-rate samples.

When asking for permission, WattDeck requests access to workouts, heart rate, active energy and cycling distance. You choose which of these to allow, and you can change or revoke your choices at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Health data is never transmitted off your device by WattDeck, and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Deleting Your Data

Individual rides can be deleted from the app's ride history by swiping on them.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Workouts already written to Apple Health belong to Health, not to WattDeck, and are removed from the Health app.

Children

WattDeck is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. It collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
